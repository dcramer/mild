# Inbox

Mild's dashboard was built to answer what Mild could not decide. Inbox makes it the place to read and handle mail too, with what Mild knows about each message beside it: its label, how sure it is, the sender's standing, and rules. It is optional. Turned on, Mild keeps a copy of the person's mail, as far back as they choose, and goes to Gmail only to change something or to fetch mail it does not have yet; and it asks Google for leave to send as them.

## What it is for

- Reading and answering mail as fast as in Gmail: a list, a reader, a composer, keys for everything.
- Seeing mail the way Mild sees it: every row says what Mild thinks it is, and the list narrows by that.
- Correcting Mild where you read, not on a separate screen: the reader every list opens has the answer and Rule… on one line under the title.

## The frame

On a desktop the sections move from the masthead to a slim column on the left (`Rail`, `web/src/system/rail.tsx`): the wordmark at its top, with Inbox on Compose under it, then one icon per section with its name under it (Inbox, Activity, Labels, Rules), the current one marked in the highlighter. Inbox takes For review's place while it is on, carrying how many wait, and `/` goes to it (`useStartsOn`, `web/src/dashboard.tsx`, and before anything is drawn `BEFORE_PAINT`, `web/src/lib/before-paint.ts`): what waits on the reader is answered where they handle their mail. At its foot: Mild's line (sync and cost, opening the same card as before), the mailbox switcher, and the avatar's menu: the switcher sits with the account whose mailboxes it chooses between, under the line, which stays for all of them. The page title sits at the top of the page, where the masthead was, and a `Notice` over it.

On a phone nothing moves from the bar and menu, which gain Inbox and the switcher, and Inbox's header has Compose (`PhoneCompose`, `web/src/views/inbox.tsx`).

## Mailboxes

A person may have several Gmail accounts connected. Everywhere a list shows mail, it is every mailbox's, unless the switcher at the foot of the rail narrows it to one. The choice is the reader's convenience, kept in the browser, and sent with every request as `mailbox` (`narrow`, `src/dashboard/scope.ts`). A row says which mailbox its mail is in only while the switcher shows all of them and there is more than one.

## The list

Inbox lists conversations, newest first, under the same periods as For review. A row is one line, as Gmail's: who (the senders of the conversation, the reader's own name as "me"), how many messages, the subject and the start of the newest text, and when, with a yellow question mark before the time where Mild looked and could not place it (Unlabelled). Unread is bold and bright, read plain and dimmer. Pointed at, the label Mild gave it (a `Meter` and the label, or Unlabelled), a dropdown that says otherwise, then Archive, Spam and Block sender take the time's place, on one bar reaching left over the text. The list has no column for the label: kept to the bar, it is never covered by the presses and the subject has the room. Rows select with a check; selected rows get Archive, Spam, Block, Mark read and Mark unread, and Label. Over the conversations, each rule Mild proposes is a row from Mild (`ProposalRow`): the rule as its subject, why as its text (`ProposalWords`, `proposalWhy`, `web/src/components/proposal.tsx`, which For review's say too), pointed at, Make a rule… (the rule dialog started on it) and Dismiss. Pressed, it opens in the list's place as a message does (`?proposal=<id>`, `ProposalReader`, `web/src/views/proposal.tsx`): the rule, why, and the sender's mail in the inbox, from the copy.

Keys, as Gmail's: J and K move, O or Enter opens, X selects, E archives, Shift+I and Shift+U mark read and unread, C writes a new message, / searches, Z undoes; in the reader R replies, A replies to all and F forwards.

Built (`web/src/views/inbox.tsx`, `app/(dashboard)/inbox/page.tsx`): the list, its views (Everything, Needs you, Sorted), the Label and Unread filters, the search, the period headings, and loading the next page as the end of the list is reached (`More`). A row is a `dense` table's, its time a `RowPresses` with the label as its `lead`; its check is `RowCheck`; selected rows put Archive, Spam, Block, Mark read and Mark unread, with E, !, Shift+B, Shift+I and Shift+U, in the search bar's place (`ListView`'s `selected`), so the rows do not move. The keys act on what is selected: X, then E. On a phone a row reads as For review's and has no check: swiped left it stops on Archive and swiped right on Read (or Unread), and taken to the edge it does it, with Undo on its toast (`Swipe`); a finger held on it selects it, and then a press selects or lets go, with how many in the top bar and Archive, Read (or Unread), Spam and Block along the bottom. The reader has the presses too. Label on selected rows is not built. What Mild thinks is the newest message's current decision, or the reader's answer about it where they gave one (an answer is a `feedback` row on the decision, not a decision of its own), and the `LabelPick` on the row's bar changes it as For review's answer does, its label's action carried out (`useListAnswer`, `web/src/components/answer.tsx`). A label that moves mail out of the inbox takes the row off the list at once and is sent once its toast goes, Undo keeping it; Mild's pass then moves it, after the answer is saved, so the row is kept off until the copy shows it gone, the feed asked a few times over about half a minute (`leftInbox`, `web/src/lib/copy.ts`), and the pass records the labels Gmail gives back so the feed has it at once. A label that keeps mail is saved at once and the row stays, so picking again is the undo. The reader's answer in Inbox is saved with `carryOut: false` and the mail stays where it is, since Archive is the reader's own press there, and it stays on the conversation. Not doing what the label does is not the exception a "Keep as …" rule is: the answer is the label with the label's own action, scored and kept as an example like any other, so it never proposes a rule. Mild's own `mild/…` label or review mark on the message is taken back, since the question it asked is answered.

## Mild's controls

What Mild adds to an inbox, in the order it costs:

1. **What it is.** Every message Mild decided has a label and a sureness. The list filters by label and by sureness (Sure, Needs you), and the views at the end of the bar are Everything, Needs you (what Mild would leave in the inbox or could not place) and Sorted (what it would move out).
2. **Ask.** Words typed into the search that are not a search ("invoices from vendors this month") become a filter, shown as the filter it made, which the reader can change. One model call a question; nothing is sent to the model but the words, the reader's today and labels, and the sender lookups the rule drafter makes too.

The model only reads. It never sends, drafts or replies, and is given no tool that does. Ask refuses while Inbox is off or no model is connected, and never runs unless pressed.

Built: Ask (`useAsk`, `web/src/components/ask.tsx`; `POST /api/mail/ask`; `createMailAsker`, `src/model/ask.ts`). Pressing Ask at the end of the search box, or Enter in it, sends the words with the reader's today to the model, which may look senders up and answers through one tool with a filter of what the list takes: senders (addresses or domains) any of which wrote a message of the conversation, a label, a view, unread, a first day, and words one message must have (`readMailFilter`, `src/triage/mail-ask.ts`): "the invoice from March about the roof" is invoice and roof, from the first of March. A sender no look-up returned is sent back once as unusable, so the filter never narrows to an address the model made up. The words become the search (`q`) and the rest goes into the address (`from`, `domain`, `since`, beside `label`, `view` and `unread`), each a chip that clears it, as any filter. What it says it cannot narrow by (what the reader replied to, whom they wrote to) is a note over the list; with nothing to narrow by, that note is all it answers. `GET /api/mail/threads` finds senders by their keyed hashes in SQL (`from`, `domain`, comma-separated) and a first day as the newest message's time (`since`). Every word must be in one message, so the model is told to give only the few that must be there and never alternatives: "invoices or receipts" cannot be said. Each call is a `mail_asks` row: the words and the answer encrypted, look-ups, model, tokens and cost.

## Search

The search finds conversations one of whose messages has every word typed, in its text, its sender's name or address, or its subject, in any order, case and accents aside ("helene" finds Hélène), each a whole word but the last, which may be the start of one ("invo" finds invoice; typed alone, from three letters). It looks through all the mail Mild keeps, not only the inbox, and so do Ask's senders and first day; Spam and Trash it leaves out, as Gmail does. A conversation found outside the inbox says so on its row, quietly before the subject: Archived, or Sent when every message of it is the person's own. Newest first, by the conversation's newest message, as the list.

The text is encrypted, so the search reads an index of it (`mail_bodies.search`, `src/store/search.ts`) made as the text is written: by the copy, sync and the pass (`recordKept`, `keepText`, `src/store/kept.ts`) and for what the person sends (`recordSent`). Each message's index is a Bloom filter: its words, its sender's and its subject's (`wordsOf`, `src/search.ts`), each whole and each start of each from three letters to six, each setting 7 bits chosen by a keyed hash (`searchHash`, `src/secrets.ts`), in a filter of about 12 bits a term rounded up to a power of two, from 128 bytes to 4 KB. A search turns its words into the same terms and asks Postgres for the conversations with a message whose filter has all their bits (`get_bit`, `mayMatch`); that is true of every message that matches and of some that do not, one in a few hundred to one in tens of thousands for each word, so the text of what it lets through is opened, newest first, and read for the words (`matches`), until a page and one match. A start longer than six letters is looked up by its first six, and one shorter than three after other words by those words alone, and each checked in the text. What it reveals: [security.md](security.md#the-search-index).

Being in the same row as the text, the index goes in the same write as the text whenever that goes: Inbox turned off, the window narrowed, retention. Text kept before there was an index has none (`search` null) and is not found until a pass indexes it: 1,000 a pass, begun by the look while any is left (`searchDue`, `indexKept`). A new key makes every index again with the text, in the rekey ([secrets.md](secrets.md)). What it costs: [runtime.md](runtime.md#what-it-costs).

## The reader

One reader for every list (`MessageReader`, `web/src/views/reader.tsx`, from the parts `Reader`, `ReaderBar`, `AnswerRow`, `MessageStack` and `ReplyBox`): opening a message shows it in the list's place, inside the frame, on the list's own route with `?message=`. The list stays on the page, hidden, so Back and Esc return to it as it was, with the focus on the row of the message last shown. Inbox opens a conversation, with `thread` beside `message` (its newest in the inbox) in the address: a thread read by scrolling the page, oldest first, at reading width. Each message is open, its head over its whole text, or folded to its sender, first line and when it came (`Heard`: the time today, then the day and the time); the account's own messages say "me". The newest and whatever was unread start open; pressing a folded message opens it in place, and pressing an open one's head folds it again, so several can be open at once. A message's text is read as it first opens and kept. Past five messages the middle folds into one "N more" row, keeping the first and the last two in sight, which opens all of it. Opening a conversation with unread mail marks it read. For review opens one message, as before.

The bar: back to the list by its name (Esc); from Inbox, Archive (E), which goes on to the next conversation, and Mark unread (Shift+U), which goes back to the list; where it is in the list, "3 of 19"; previous and next (K and J). The subject is the page's heading, and under it Mild's row is about the newest message: the answer, which starts on the reader's own where they gave one, and its agree check, so answering moves on in one press as For review's rows do; Why, which shows Mild's reason and, in Inbox, the history; and Rule…. Inbox has nothing beside the conversation: the sender's details are their name, a link to their page. Every other list puts What Mild saw, the sender, what else of theirs waits and the history in a column beside it, and opens Why from the start, since answering is the job there.

Under the conversation, Reply (R), Reply all (A) and Forward (F) answer the newest message in the inbox. Each open message has its own, for answering it instead: Reply, and Reply all and Forward in a menu beside it, at the end of its head. Either way the reply box opens at the foot of the conversation, in place of the presses under it, and says which message it answers when that is not the newest ("Replying to Ada Lovelace, Nov 18"). The conversation is the messages Mild recorded: with Inbox on, all of it within the window, what the person sent from Gmail or a phone among it, once the copy has reached it.

## The composer

One composer, in two places (`web/src/views/composer.tsx`): new mail in a narrow panel of its own (`Composer`), opened by C or the rail's Compose on a desktop and Inbox's Compose on a phone; a reply or a forward in the reader, at the foot of the conversation, which stays above it (`InlineComposer`, in a `ReplyBox`), opened by Reply, Reply all and Forward under it or on any open message. Each opens through `useCompose()` (`web/src/lib/compose.ts`): `compose()` for new mail, `compose({ kind: 'reply', messageId, all })` or `compose({ kind: 'forward', messageId })`. Where it is open is in the address: `?compose=new` adds a history entry, so Back closes it; `reply`, `all` or `forward` rewrites the reader's own, so Back goes to the list and the draft waits for the next Reply; it answers the reader's message (`?message=<id>`), or with `answering=<id>` another of its conversation, so a reload keeps the box on the message it answers. Sent, the reply box closes with the focus back on the reader and the reply in the conversation. With Inbox off nothing offers it, and the address opens nothing.

- **From**, when there is more than one address to send as: every mailbox Google lets Mild send from and its aliases (`aliases` in `/api/manage`), and the one it starts on even if it may not yet. New mail starts on the mailbox the switch shows (the primary for All); a reply or a forward on the mailbox its message came to, by the address it was sent to, and offers only that mailbox's addresses.
- **To, Cc and Bcc**: each recipient a chip (`Recipients`, `web/src/system/recipients.tsx`), its name, with the address in a tooltip. Typed as `ada@example.org` or `Ada <ada@example.org>`, an entry becomes a chip on a comma, semicolon, Enter or Tab, on leaving the line, or on Send, which takes what is still being typed; pasted, a list becomes chips at once, each address once. One that is not an address is underlined in red, said under the line, and keeps Send waiting; pressed, it goes back to be typed. Backspace at the start takes the last chip off. Cc and Bcc are each behind a press, Cc open from the start on a reply to all. Bcc goes in the message Gmail is given, which delivers to it and takes it off what everyone receives; the record keeps it, as Gmail's Sent copy does.
- **Suggestions** as an address is typed, after a pause (`GET /api/mail/addresses?q=`): everyone in the person's address book (`addresses`, `src/store/addresses.ts`) each word typed is the start of a word of whose name or address, those they have written to first and most recently, then those who wrote to them, eight at most. The book is filled as mail is recorded (`insertMessages`, `recordKept`, so what the person sends too): whoever the person sent mail to, To, Cc and Bcc, and the sender of any mail that was not sent in bulk or marked spam, never the person's own addresses, with the newest name seen and never a name given up for none. Mail recorded before there was a book the pass adds, oldest first, 5,000 messages a pass (`fillAddresses`, from `mailboxes.addresses_from`). Addresses are sealed and found by keyed hash like the rest, so each row carries a keyed Bloom filter of the starts of its words, one letter to six (`addressTerms`, `bloomOf`): the database narrows by it, and the 200 best it lets through are opened and checked. A new key empties the book, and the pass fills it again. A reply starts To on whoever replies go to (Reply-To, else the sender; mail the person sent, to whoever it went to), and to all with everyone else on it in Cc, the person's own addresses left out.
- **Subject** for new mail and a forward, which starts on its message's with "Fwd: "; a reply's is its message's with "Re: ", shown under the title and set by the server.
- **The text**, plain, at reading size. A forward's starts with its message quoted as Gmail quotes one (From, Date, Subject, To, Cc, then its text), with the reader's place above it. It goes as new mail, a conversation of its own, with the message's files, each of which can be taken off.
- **Files**, under the text (`DraftFiles`, `web/src/system/attachments.tsx`), added by the paperclip beside Send or by dropping them on the composer, 25 MB in all, as Gmail sends. Each is uploaded as it is added, in pieces of 3 MB (`uploadFile`, `web/src/lib/upload.ts`), since Vercel takes at most 4.5 MB in a request, and held, encrypted, in `uploads` until Send names it (`POST /api/mail/upload`, `/api/mail/upload/piece`, `/api/mail/upload/remove`); the draft keeps the ids of those that have uploaded, so a reload keeps them (one still uploading is lost), and Send waits while one is uploading. One taken off is removed from Mild, even as it finishes. A forward's files are the message's: their names, types, sizes and Gmail's ids for them are kept with its text at the first whole read (`mail_bodies.attachments`), and their bytes fetched from Gmail as it is sent (`forwardedFiles`, `src/work/attachments.ts`), never kept. Mail Inbox kept before Mild kept these lists is read from Gmail once, as its forward opens (`POST /api/mail/attachments`), and kept; an id Gmail no longer takes is read again once with the message, for the ids it has now.

Send is the one `ink` press, with ⌘↵ or Ctrl ↵ from anywhere in it; Discard drops the draft, with Undo on its toast. Sent, it closes and a toast says `Sent` under the subject; a refusal or failure keeps it open with the words under the presses. A mailbox Google has not let Mild send from says so, with the press that asks (`/auth/login?grant=send&mailbox=…&to=<this page>`), and Send waits. What is written is kept in this browser (`localStorage`, under who is signed in: one draft for new mail, one per message answered and one per message forwarded) until it is sent or discarded, or they sign out, with the Message-ID of a press of Send that may not have gone.

## Turning it on

Settings has Inbox, off by default (`Mail`, `web/src/views/settings.tsx`), and so does the Inbox screen while it is off, as a `Callout` (`TurnInboxOn`, `web/src/components/inbox-switch.tsx`); Inbox is among the sections only while it is on. Each asks first how far back to keep the mail (`MailWindowDialog`, `web/src/components/inbox-switch.tsx`): the last year, the last three years, or all of it, each with about how many messages, what they take to keep and how long copying them takes (`GET /api/mail/reach`). While on, Settings shows the copy going (counting what it will copy, then how far of that it has got and about how long is left) or the window kept, and Change… opens the same choice: wider copies what it adds, narrower asks first (a `DeleteButton`) and deletes the text outside it. Turning it off deletes what Mild kept for it, and nothing offers to send. Turning it on does not ask Google for `gmail.send`: the composer asks, for the mailbox it would send from, when it may not yet ([TODO.md](../TODO.md)).

## Data

Inbox is off until the person turns it on (`preferences.mail`, `POST /api/mail/on`, `mail` in `/api/manage`).

| | Where | While |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is in the inbox now, and what is unread | `messages.label_ids`, kept from Gmail's history by every sync (`followLabels`, `src/store/messages.ts`); `messages_inbox` lists it newest first, `messages_thread` finds a thread | Always, for everyone. Stale for what changed while the history cursor had expired |
| Whether Mild may send from a mailbox | `mailboxes.can_send`, from the scopes of the sign-in it holds, set at every sign-in; `canSend` per mailbox in `/api/manage`. `/auth/login?grant=send&mailbox=<address>` (and `&to=<path>` to come back to) asks Google for it | Until a sign-in without it replaces the token |
| The person's mail | A `messages` row each, and its whole text and HTML in `mail_bodies`, sealed like `read_bodies` (`src/store/content.ts`): mail received within the window (`preferences.mail_window`: `year`, `three_years`, `all`), and whatever is in the inbox however old | Inbox is on. Turning it off deletes every row of `mail_bodies` and `mail_copies` in the same write; the records then go after a year, as any record does |
| Mail the person sent | A `messages` row with Gmail's id, conversation and labels for it, decided already so it is never triaged, and what they wrote in `mail_bodies`: from Mild by whichever try of the send finds it went (`recordSent`, `src/store/sent.ts`), from Gmail or a phone by the sync that reads it gaining `SENT` | While Inbox is on |

Three things fill it, each recording a message and its text in one transaction under a share lock on the person's preferences, so turning Inbox off waits for a write under way and its delete finds it, and nothing lands after it (`recordKept`, `keepText`, `src/store/kept.ts`):

- **The copy** (`copyMail`, `src/work/copy.ts`; `mail_copies`, one row a mailbox). Turning Inbox on starts one for each mailbox, and connecting a mailbox while it is on starts its own. It lists `{in:inbox after:<since>} before:<before> -in:drafts -in:chats` (Gmail takes epoch seconds), newest first: first 500 ids a page only to count them (`countCopy`, `src/store/copies.ts`), since the `resultSizeEstimate` of this search is no guide to how many there are, then 100 a page, and reads each message it has no record of whole once (`getMessage`, `format=full`, 60 units: headers, labels and text in one read, where metadata then text would be 80), recording it decided already, so it is never in the triage queue; going through the inbox still decides what of it is in the inbox, as a press. A message recorded without its text has its text read (60); one in the inbox and recorded is left to the pass, which keeps inbox text newest first (`keepMailBodies`), so the two never read the same message. Spam, Trash, drafts and chats are not mail (`NOT_MAIL`, `src/gmail/sync.ts`). The page's token is kept once its messages are, so a step cut off at its limit lists the same page again and passes over what it recorded: safe to repeat, and a crash loses nothing. It is a run of its own a mailbox (`copy:<mailbox's number>`), begun by each tick while Inbox is on and the sign-in has not expired; several mailboxes copy side by side, each under its own quota. `before` is when Inbox was turned on: sync records what came after.
- **Sync, wider.** While Inbox is on, history's new mail outside the inbox (a filter's, mail archived as it arrived) and mail gaining `SENT` (what the person sent from Gmail or a phone) is read whole and recorded the same way, untriaged, and begins a pass as new mail does (`elsewhere`, `pullChanges`). New inbox mail is read whole too, once (60 units, not 20 for its headers and 60 more for its text), recorded and triaged as before, and its text kept as it is recorded, so the model reads it from there.
- **The pass**, for inbox mail it has a record of and no text: 50 a pass after triage, taken from what Mild read with Inbox off in the last 30 days (`read_bodies`) before Gmail, and anything a pass reads from Gmail is kept here as it is read (`keepMailBodies`, `readingOnce`, `src/work/bodies.ts`); the look begins a pass while some is missing (`mailBodiesDue`).

The window moves with time: "the last year" keeps a year back from today, and retention lets go of what falls out of it ([architecture.md](architecture.md), Retention). Widening it starts a copy from the new start to the old (or moves the copy under way back to it); narrowing deletes the text outside it in the same write, stops a copy under way at the new start, and leaves the records to the next upkeep. The text is all of it up to 200,000 characters (`MAX_MAIL_CHARS`, `src/gmail/body.ts`), not the 4,000 the model is shown, and the model and the other tiers read it from here instead of Gmail (`heldText`), and the search from its index (Search, above). What it costs: [runtime.md](runtime.md#what-it-costs).

The list is `GET /api/mail/threads` (`listThreads`, `src/dashboard/queries/mail.ts`): conversations with a message in the inbox now, by `messages_inbox`, or for a search or Ask's senders or first day every conversation Mild keeps, all their recorded messages by `messages_thread`, 50 a page from `before` (the last one's newest time and thread). `view`, `label` and `unread` narrow it in SQL. A search (`q`) reads through at most 200 conversations the index lets through after `before`, and answers what matched, with `next` to read on from. A conversation is `GET /api/mail/thread?thread=<id>` (`conversation`), found in whichever of the person's mailboxes holds it as `/api/message` finds a message, and a message's whole text is `/api/message`'s, which reads `mail_bodies` before the copies For review keeps. Neither reads Gmail.

The reader's own presses are `POST /api/mail/change` (`{ accountId, messageIds, change: 'archive' | 'spam' | 'read' | 'unread' }`), through `Controls.changeMail` to the executor's `press` (`src/actions/executor.ts`): only a message in the inbox now, its labels read again first, the change written to `actions` before the call (`archive` or `spam` with no decision, `read`, `unread`) and checked against Gmail's answer, whose labels go straight into `messages.label_ids`, as every label the executor reads or is answered with does, Mild's own changes included: sync begins no pass for those, so the list would otherwise keep mail Mild archived until other mail came (`labelsNow`). They act whether Mild is on or not, as an answer does: they are the person's own, not Mild's. So they carry no `mild/did/` label, a correction never takes them back (`lastStandingChange` is Mild's alone), and they are no answer to learn from. Undo on the toast is the executor's undo of each change, which puts the labels in the record back too. Spam is the reader's own word, so it is not refused as Mild's is for a sender they have written to. Block gives each sender the Blocked standing, as their page's Block does (`POST /api/standing`), and marks their conversation spam, with one Undo for both; where their own rule says more than Block would, nothing is given and the rule dialog opens on it instead. Gmail is asked twice a message: a quota unit of 5 for the read, 5 for the change.

The page sends through `POST /api/mail/send` (`SendRequest`, `src/dashboard/types.ts`), which takes the mailbox, the Message-ID the page made for the press, From, To, Cc, Bcc, the text and the files uploaded for it, and either a subject (new mail and a forward, with the parts of the forwarded message's files that go) or the message it answers. For a reply the route reads that message's record (`replyTo`, `src/dashboard/queries/reply.ts`) for its conversation, its subject with "Re: ", and the Message-IDs it follows (its own, after its References, at most 20 kept: the first and the newest), and the page's word on any of them is not asked for. It hands that to `Controls.send`: `sendMail` (`src/actions/send.ts`) builds plain text in UTF-8 from `{ from, to, cc, bcc, subject, text, inReplyTo, references }`, with the files after it as `multipart/mixed`, each named in quoted ASCII and in RFC 2231's encoding and of a type it can trust, refusing a header value that could break a line and any address that is not plain, and sends it through `Mailbox.send`, by Gmail's upload address, which takes mail up to 35 MB. The files are fetched only once an earlier try is known not to have gone, and the uploads removed once it has. It refuses unless Inbox is on, the mailbox may send and `from` is one of its addresses. The page makes a press's Message-ID when Send is first pressed, keeps it with the draft until the draft changes, and gives it again on a retry, which first looks for the earlier try in Gmail, as a digest does. Gmail's search can lag a send by seconds, so while an earlier try is still waiting on Gmail, or failed less than two minutes ago (`SETTLE_MS`) and is not found, the retry is refused rather than sent. Once Gmail has taken it, the press succeeds even if writing it to the record fails: sync records it later, and an error there would invite a second send. Each try is an `actions` row of type `send`, written before the call; a send cannot be undone.
